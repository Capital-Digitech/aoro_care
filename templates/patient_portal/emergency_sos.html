What Happens When You Send SOS

  • An alert is recorded with your latest heart rate and SpO₂ from your health ring.
  • Your assigned doctor is notified, and the alert appears in their Emergency Alerts.
  • Linked family members who have alerts turned on are notified too.
  • When you are safe, mark the alert as “I'm Safe” so everyone knows.
